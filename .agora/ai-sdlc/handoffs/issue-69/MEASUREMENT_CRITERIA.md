# Measurement criteria

## MC-069-01 - AI-native product definition

Review `PRODUCT_INTENT.md`, `PEDAGOGY.md` and `LEARNER_JOURNEY.md` for language that treats AI primarily as optional stuck-path help. Passing evidence shows AI as transparent scaffolding inside the normal learner loop.

## MC-069-02 - Scratch plus chatbot differentiation

A reviewer can summarize at least three differentiators: visible proposal/acceptance boundary, runtime evidence authority, continuous block/code visibility, learner explanation/reflection, and provider-independent learning companion constraints.

## MC-069-03 - Testable learner agency

The documents define observable product states for proposed change, accepted program and executed result, plus allowed learner actions to accept, modify or reject AI suggestions.

## MC-069-04 - Runtime authority

The documents state that deterministic runtime observations and completion predicates outrank AI claims for execution behavior.

## MC-069-05 - Hidden mutation prohibition

The documents explicitly prohibit hidden AI code generation or program mutation and require learner-visible inspection before accepted changes affect the canonical program.

## MC-069-06 - Complete learner loop

The learner journey includes intent, clarification/decomposition, bounded proposal, learner decision, visible blocks/code, prediction, execution, observation, evidence-grounded debugging, improvement and explanation/reflection.

## MC-069-07 - Terminology consistency

Terms for tutor, learning companion, AI proposal, runtime evidence and learner explanation are consistent across the three source-of-truth documents, with `CONTENT_GUIDE.md` aligned only where necessary.

## MC-069-08 - Open decisions

Any unresolved product or architecture boundary found during editing is recorded in the docs or PR evidence rather than resolved silently by the agent.
