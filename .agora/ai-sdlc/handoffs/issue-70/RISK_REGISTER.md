# Risk Register

## RISK-070-01 - Age-gating replaces competency

Mitigation: define stages by observable behavior and autonomy, not age.

## RISK-070-02 - AI completes before learner reasons

Mitigation: every stage defines what AI should avoid and what counts as over-assistance.

## RISK-070-03 - Code becomes hidden during progression

Mitigation: require continuous visible block/text relationship from #69 and `PROGRAMMING_MODEL.md`.

## RISK-070-04 - AI literacy becomes prompt engineering

Mitigation: frame AI literacy around evidence, fallibility, privacy and learner decision-making.

## RISK-070-05 - Understanding cannot be observed

Mitigation: every stage includes observable evidence of understanding.
