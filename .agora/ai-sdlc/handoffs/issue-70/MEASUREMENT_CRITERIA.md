# Measurement Criteria

## MC-070-01 - Concept-based progression

The document defines stages by capabilities and observable behavior, not age.

## MC-070-02 - Coverage matrix

Programming, human-AI collaboration and AI literacy dimensions are represented across stages.

## MC-070-03 - Scaffolding rubric completeness

Every stage has AI may/avoid, learner action, evidence, escalation/de-escalation and over-assistance.

## MC-070-04 - AI autonomy gradient

AI responsibility decreases as learner autonomy increases.

## MC-070-05 - Review challenges

The document enables review of whether code is hidden, AI completes before reasoning or understanding is unobservable.
