# Requirements: Issue #70 learning progression

## R1 - Concept-based stages

The progression must not hard-code age as a technical gate. Stages must be based on observable competency and autonomy.

## R2 - Programming coverage

Cover sequence, events, repetition, conditions, state/variables, decomposition/functions, debugging, reading code and later textual-code modification.

## R3 - Human-AI collaboration coverage

Cover expressing intent, clarifying questions, proposal inspection, accept/reject/modify, prediction, testing proposals, challenging AI, comparing alternatives and explaining decisions.

## R4 - AI literacy coverage

Cover that AI can be wrong, fluent language is not proof, runtime/test evidence matters, models can disagree, private data is unnecessary and the learner remains author/decision maker.

## R5 - Scaffolding rubric

Each stage must define what AI may do, what AI should avoid, expected learner action, observable evidence, escalation/de-escalation and over-assistance.

## R6 - Provider independence

The progression must not depend on a specific model/provider or frame learning as prompt engineering.
