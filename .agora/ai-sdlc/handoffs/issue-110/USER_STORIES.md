## User Stories

- US-001: As a child learner, I can choose English or Spanish so the app speaks the language I understand best.
- US-002: As a child learner, I can switch language during a mission without losing my work or changing what my program does.
- US-003: As a child learner, I can complete the First Mission in English or Spanish with localized goals, hints, feedback, and reflection prompts.
- US-004: As a child learner, I see run controls, proposal review text, and execution evidence labels in my selected language.
- US-005: As a child learner, the Learning Companion responds in my selected product language.
- US-006: As a parent or educator, I can trust that safety-critical guidance is present in both supported languages.
- US-007: As a developer, I can add a future third locale through catalogs and registry entries without touching canonical program/runtime semantics.
- US-008: As a developer, I get a clear failure or diagnostic when a translation key is missing.
- US-009: As a contributor, I can read translation guidance and keep English and Spanish product docs aligned.
