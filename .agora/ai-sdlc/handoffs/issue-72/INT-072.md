# INT-072 - Transparent AI-native architecture invariants

## Intent interpretation

Issue #72 updates repository execution and architecture contracts so future human or AI developers cannot reintroduce the old hidden-assistant model. The work is documentation/source-of-truth: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/architecture/SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`, and a replacement or clear supersession of `docs/architecture/AI_TUTOR.md`.

## Source facts

- Product north star: AI proposes. Child decides. Runtime proves. Child explains.
- Product rule: nothing happens under the rug.
- AI-originated programming suggestions are proposals until the learner accepts, rejects or modifies them.
- Canonical program state is the authority for editing and execution.
- Runtime evidence is the authority for behavior and mission completion.
- Generated text/language views are learning projections and must not become independent source or arbitrary executed provider output.
- Provider identity must not leak into domain contracts.
- Child privacy and safety override personalization, engagement and AI convenience.

## Proposed decision

Supersede broad "AI tutor" terminology in architecture docs with "Learning Companion" for the product capability. Retain "tutor-api" only as a legacy/package name where changing source package names is out of scope for this documentation issue, and explicitly define it as an adapter boundary rather than product authority.

## Material clarifications

No blocking clarification is required before Inception approval. The issue explicitly requires the invariants, boundaries, deliverables and evidence type.

## Human decision required

Product Owner approval is required to enter Construction and update the durable docs.
