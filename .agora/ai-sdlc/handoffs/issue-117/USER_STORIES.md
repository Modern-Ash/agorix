## User Stories

- US-001: As a learner, I experience Agorix as a calm creative product that still feels capable as I get older.
- US-002: As a learner, I can distinguish action categories, runtime evidence, AI proposals and errors without relying only on color.
- US-003: As a tablet learner, I get touch-sized controls and layouts that do not depend on hover.
- US-004: As a Studio learner, I see a coherent dark IDE surface that still feels like Agorix.
- US-005: As a designer/developer, I can implement Web/Tablet and Studio visuals from source-controlled tokens and component rules.
- US-006: As a contributor, I can extend the design system for future Worlds or language packs without copying private design context.
- US-007: As a Spanish-speaking learner, longer localized text fits the component guidance without breaking the layout.
