## Proposed Units

### UOW-117-1: Visual foundations

Define principles, anti-goals, tokens, semantic color model, typography, spacing, radii, elevation, motion and iconography.

### UOW-117-2: Cross-surface component grammar

Define the required components and their light/dark, touch, accessibility and state behavior across Web/Tablet and Studio.

### UOW-117-3: Examples, accessibility and verification

Add tablet landscape, tablet portrait and Studio dark examples; verify acceptance trace and formatting.
